## 🌾 The Problem

India has **140 million farmers.**
Most of them don't know:
- 📉 When to sell crops for **maximum profit**
- 🌱 Which crop gives **best income** this season
- 💬 Where to get **farming advice in Hindi**
- 📊 How to **track and grow** their income

Existing solutions need drones and sensors 
worth ₹50,000+.

**Most farmers only have a smartphone.**

---

## 💡 The Solution

**MandiBuddy** — A personal AI market analyst
in every farmer's pocket.

> No drones. No sensors. No budget needed.
> Just a smartphone and MandiBuddy.

---

## 🚀 4 AI Modules

| Module | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| 💰 **PriceSense** | Predicts mandi prices 30 days ahead |
| 📊 **IncomePath** | Tracks and grows farmer income |
| 🤖 **KisanBot** | Hindi + English AI farming assistant |
| 🌾 **CropAdvisor** | Best crops — zero hardware needed |
